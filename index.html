Why people switch

One link, one list

Share a single link. Everyone adds their name or item to the same list, so the chat doesn’t fill with copies.

Smart sharing

The creator can re-share only after the list has changed, and people who open a link can’t re-post it. No repeat spam.

Stays out of your chat

Your group keeps its conversation. The list lives on its own page, not buried under 200 messages.

No sign-up, free always

Open it, type a title, start a list. No account, no app to install.

Tick, number, tidy
